Privacy Policy – aomsonai report

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses read-only Google scopes (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read statistics for the owner's own YouTube channel: views, watch time, subscribers, likes, comments, traffic sources, countries and aggregated age/gender percentages.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce a private daily report for the channel owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app never posts, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.

Storage

Reports and the OAuth refresh token are stored privately by the channel owner. Data about individual viewers is never collected; YouTube Analytics only provides aggregated figures.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
